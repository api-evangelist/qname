# QName AI

> QName AI is a bilingual domain-research workspace for AI SaaS builders. It combines AI domain discovery, WHOIS and RDAP evidence, domain age checks, bulk research, model news, and revenue intelligence.

Canonical site: https://qname.ai/
Language versions: English at https://qname.ai/ and Simplified Chinese under https://qname.ai/zh/
Support: hello@qname.ai

## Core product pages

- [AI domain search](https://qname.ai/): Search a naming stem across relevant extensions and review domain evidence.
- [WHOIS domain lookup](https://qname.ai/whois): Inspect registrar, registration dates, nameservers, status codes, and raw WHOIS or RDAP evidence.
- [Bulk WHOIS](https://qname.ai/bulk-whois): Clean and check domain lists in batches and export structured results.
- [Domain Sweep](https://qname.ai/domain-sweep): Generate candidates from prefixes, dictionaries, and suffixes before a bulk lookup.
- [Domain age checker](https://qname.ai/age): Calculate age from the reported registration creation date while preserving source context.
- [AI domain agent](https://qname.ai/agent): Chat with Domain Claw to turn product ideas into naming candidates and continue into WHOIS research.
- [AI model news](https://qname.ai/ai-model-news): Dated model and category signals used as research inputs for product and domain discovery.
- [Revenue intelligence](https://qname.ai/revenue): Evidence-led analysis of AI SaaS products using time-stamped third-party revenue signals.

## Interpretation notes

- QName AI is a research product, not a domain registrar, trademark database, legal adviser, or guarantee of availability.
- WHOIS and RDAP fields vary by registry, registrar, extension, privacy policy, and cache freshness. Missing data should be treated as unknown.
- Domain age is calculated from a reported creation date and does not prove ownership continuity, clean history, traffic, authority, or search rankings.
- AI model and revenue signals are time-stamped research inputs. Reopen the cited primary or third-party source before relying on a current fact.
